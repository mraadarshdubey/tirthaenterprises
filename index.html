SIP

What does a SIP actually mean?

A Systematic Investment Plan is a method of investing a fixed amount periodically into a Mutual Fund scheme. Understanding how it works, the market risks involved and its role in long-term planning can help you evaluate whether it fits your requirements.

Mutual Funds What are you actually investing in?

Mutual Funds pool money from multiple investors and invest according to the scheme’s stated objective. Different schemes involve different objectives and risks.

Time Horizon Why does time matter?

RISK IS PART OF THE CONVERSATION

Understand the opportunity.
Understand the risk.

Mutual Fund investments are market-linked. Different schemes can involve different levels and types of risk. A responsible financial conversation should include an understanding of both the goal and the risks involved.

Clarity does not remove risk. It helps you understand it.
